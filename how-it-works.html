Clear Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Does connecting Qlarity alter our live ad campaigns or budgets?

Never. Qlarity requests strictly read-only OAuth 2.0 access. We can never alter your campaign budgets, bids, pauses, or ad creatives. You maintain 100% control over your ad accounts at all times.

How fast does data sync from Google Ads and Meta?

Lead scores are computed in real time (within 500ms of form submission or webhook reception). Ad spend, impressions, clicks, and campaign metrics sync automatically on an hourly cron schedule, with 1-click manual refresh anytime.

Is our client data compliant with India's DPDP Act?

Yes. All lead PII (email, phone) is encrypted using AES-256 at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit. For attribution tracking and conversion feedback, sensitive data is cryptographically hashed using SHA-256 in strict accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act.
