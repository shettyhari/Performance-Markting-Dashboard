★ Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

  • ✓ The Algorithm Blindspot: When you tell Meta or Google to "get more leads", the algorithm targets users with high form-completion propensity, which often correlates with retirees, job seekers, and bots.

  • ✓ Value-Based Bidding (VBB): Rather than passing a generic conversion event, sending an AI-computed quality score (1–100) via Meta CAPI enables Target ROAS bidding on lead generation campaigns.

1. The “Garbage In, Garbage Out” Dilemma of Ad Algorithms

Machine learning recommendation engines like Meta’s Andromeda and Google’s Smart Bidding are marvels of engineering. They process thousands of real-time signals—device type, time of day, historical browsing activity—to predict user behavior.

However, their objective function is simple: maximize the target conversion you specify at the lowest cost.

If you define a conversion as a completed form submit on a Meta Instant Form or website thank-you page, the algorithm optimizes to find people who fill out forms. But in emerging digital markets like India, millions of casual users tap pre-filled Facebook forms by mistake or submit fake contact details to bypass content gates. The algorithm views these as triumphs, sending you more of the same.

2. The Anatomy of an AI Lead Scoring Engine

To break this negative cycle, Qlarity utilizes a multi-layer AI scoring pipeline that evaluates every inbound lead across 20+ real-time attributes:

Layer 1: Identity & Deliverability Verification

Checks if email domain has active MX records, detects disposable/temporary email providers (e.g., Mailinator, TempMail), verifies Indian mobile number formats (valid 10-digit prefixes, telecommunication circle validation), and flags repetitive submission patterns.

Layer 2: Commercial Intent & Firmographics

Analyzes corporate vs. personal domain status (e.g. @company.com vs. @gmail.com for B2B), matches company domain against firmographic databases for employee headcount and revenue tier, and verifies job title seniority.

Layer 3: Behavioral Intent Signals

Evaluates landing page interaction metrics: time on page before form fill, scroll depth, session frequency, and referring search keyword intent (e.g., “pricing / enterprise solution” vs “free tutorial”).

3. Pushing Quality Signals Back into Meta CAPI and Google Ads

Once a lead is scored, the score should not simply sit in your CRM. It must be transformed into an advertising signal:

The Value-Based Bidding Workflow:

  1. Leads with Score < 40 (Spam/Disqualified): Excluded from conversion feedback loops or flagged as negative conversion events so the algorithm ceases targeting similar audiences.
  2. Leads with Score 40–70 (MQL): Sent as standard conversion events.
  3. Leads with Score 70–100 (High-Intent SQL): Sent via Meta Conversions API (CAPI) with an assigned monetary conversion value (e.g., ₹5,000 or $100).

With value-based bidding enabled, the ad auction algorithm redirects budget toward prospects who match the profile of your highest-scoring buyers.
