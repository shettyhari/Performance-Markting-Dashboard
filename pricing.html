Frequently Asked Questions About Pricing

We manage ad accounts in USD, AED, and GBP. Do we pay extra?

No! That is the core advantage of Qlarity. Unlike US platforms that charge exorbitant foreign transaction fees and seat costs, Qlarity lets your agency track unlimited global client ad spends under a single, cost-effective INR plan.

Can we deduct TDS under Section 194J?

Yes. We issue standard Indian corporate invoices supporting TDS deductions. You can deduct tax under Section 194J and provide Form 16A certificates quarterly.
