How the CPQL Formula Actually Works

Standard ad platforms optimize for Cost Per Lead (CPL). The problem? The ad algorithm doesn't care whether the lead is a 14-year-old student, a bot using a temporary disposable email, or a legitimate enterprise VP.

Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPQL) = Total Ad Spend / (Total Leads × Qualification Rate)

When Qlarity scores leads in real time and automatically pushes qualified conversion milestones back through Meta CAPI and Google Enhanced Conversions, the ad algorithms learn to stop serving impressions to junk profiles and focus strictly on prospects matching verified buyer behavior.
