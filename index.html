FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is Qlarity?

Qlarity is a performance marketing analytics dashboard that brings advertising metrics and CRM lead outcomes together. It helps teams measure campaign performance, track cost per qualified lead, compare brands, and investigate lead quality.

How is Qlarity different from Meta Ads Manager or Google Ads?

Advertising platforms report the metrics they collect within their own systems. Qlarity adds a broader view by bringing advertising data together with CRM lead statuses and sales outcomes, helping you assess performance beyond the initial conversion.

Can I track cost per qualified lead?

Yes. Qlarity calculates cost per qualified lead using advertising spend and the number of leads marked as qualified according to your team's configured definitions.

Does Qlarity support Meta Ads and Google Ads?

Yes. Upload Meta Ads and Google Ads reports, or connect Meta Ads and Google Ads directly with your own access in Settings so the numbers refresh automatically.

Which CRMs can I connect to Qlarity?

Any CRM that can export leads or send them by webhook, for example TeleCRM, Zoho CRM, HubSpot, LeadSquared, Salesforce and Pipedrive. A ready-made workflow for TeleCRM is included.

Can I manage multiple brands in one dashboard?

Yes. Qlarity is designed to help teams compare campaign performance across multiple brands and review metrics at the brand, campaign, ad set and ad levels.

Can Qlarity automate marketing reports?

Yes. Qlarity provides daily, weekly and monthly reporting, along with Telegram alerts for configured performance changes and ad account issues.

Does Qlarity have AI-powered analytics?

Yes. Ask AI lets you ask questions about the performance data in your dashboard. Your workspace owner chooses the AI provider (Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT, Google Gemini or a compatible service) and adds its key.

Is Qlarity suitable for digital marketing agencies?

Yes. Agencies and consultants can use Qlarity to organise reporting, compare advertising metrics with CRM outcomes, and communicate lead quality and campaign performance more clearly, with a separate workspace for each client.

How much does Qlarity cost?

Qlarity is free during early access. No payment card is required.

Do I need technical knowledge to use Qlarity?

No. Begin by creating a workspace, configuring your brands, and uploading your advertising and CRM reports. API connections and webhook workflows take a little more setup.
