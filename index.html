FAQ

Questions, answered

What is cost per qualified lead (CPQL)?

CPQL is your ad spend divided by the number of leads your team marked as qualified in the CRM. Unlike cost per lead, it ignores junk, duplicate and unreachable leads, so it shows what an interested lead really costs.

Which CRMs does Qlarity work with?

Any CRM that can export leads to a spreadsheet or send them by webhook, for example TeleCRM, Zoho CRM, HubSpot, LeadSquared, Salesforce or Pipedrive. Upload an export, or push new leads automatically; a ready-made n8n workflow for TeleCRM is included.

Do I need a developer to set it up?

No. Create a workspace, add your brands, upload your Meta report and CRM export, and the dashboard is ready. Connecting the Meta API or a CRM webhook for automatic updates takes a few minutes in Settings.

Where is my data stored and who can see it?

In India, on Amazon Web Services in Mumbai, on an encrypted disk with nightly backups. Every company works in its own workspace, and the database itself keeps each workspace's data separate. Ad and AI tokens are stored encrypted. See the Privacy Policy.

Which AI does Ask AI use?

The one your workspace owner chooses: Anthropic Claude, OpenAI GPT, Google Gemini or any OpenAI-compatible service, with your own API key. It answers only from your dashboard's numbers and never sees your leads' names, phone numbers or email addresses.

How much does Qlarity cost?

Qlarity is free during early access. If pricing is introduced later, workspace owners get at least 30 days' notice first.
