Attribution Science

5 Multi-Touch Attribution Models Out of the Box

Buyer journeys are rarely linear. A prospect might discover your brand through a Google Search ad, engage with an educational LinkedIn carousel, and finally convert on a Meta retargeting ad. Qlarity lets you evaluate revenue under every lens:

1. First-Touch Attribution

Gives 100% of the credit to the initial discovery click. Perfect for measuring which top-of-funnel channels drive net-new brand awareness.

2. Last-Touch Attribution

Attributes 100% credit to the final touchpoint before form submission. Essential for evaluating closing and retargeting efficiency.

3. Linear Attribution

Distributes credit equally across all touchpoints in the prospect's journey. Prevents under-valuing mid-funnel content.

4. Position-Based (U-Shaped)

Allocates 40% credit to the first touch, 40% to the lead conversion touch, and spreads the remaining 20% across intermediate touchpoints.

5. Time-Decay Attribution

Gives more weight to touchpoints that occurred closest in time to the conversion event, reflecting immediate purchase momentum.
